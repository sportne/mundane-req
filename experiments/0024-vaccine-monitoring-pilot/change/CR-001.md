# Change Request CR-001: Extend Hosted-Data Period

Status: Approved for pilot execution

Requested: 2026-08-30

Authority: Pilot employer role, exercised by the pilot systems engineer

## Requested change

Change the contracted minimum period for hosted access to SVEMS monitoring data
from three years to five years. The employer wants one service period to cover a
longer programme audit and investigation horizon.

## Scope constraints

- The change affects hosted availability, retained record lifecycle, and the
  verification objective for retention.
- It does not alter acquisition intervals, local device storage, alarm timing,
  notification policy, data ownership, or the source-language contract.
- Existing data at the time of deployment is assumed to have a remaining
  retention period no shorter than the new five-year rule measured from each
  record's recorded time.

## Acceptance

The changed baseline shall state five years consistently, retain trace links and
complete verification coverage, pass the formatter and validator, and record
all baseline-bound companion updates exposed by the change.
