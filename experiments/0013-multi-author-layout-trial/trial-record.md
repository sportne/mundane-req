## Supplied material and task text

The starting subject layout was the exact 60-requirement corpus at the protocol
commit. The one-record layout was constructed by the trial harness from that
source and accepted only after its complete semantic fingerprint equaled the
subject layout. The four exact task pairs are the frozen TC-0703 scenarios in
[`trial-protocol.md`](../0011-operational-corpus/trial-protocol.md); no task text,
corpus source, grammar, or tool was changed during execution.

This was a deterministic controlled simulation. `Author A` and `Author B` are
distinct Git identities on independent branches from the same scenario
baseline. They are not human participants. No assistance was supplied, no live
forge was used, and no source-changing formatter mode was invoked.

- exact validator counts and trace output for both branches and the final merge;

state, exact conflicts, exact validator counts, full semantic equality against

clean final worktrees, and the observed file/conflict shape.

There were no validator diagnostics or participant questions because every
authored branch was valid on its first tool run. Successful command output and
empty diagnostic streams are recorded rather than interpreted as human
learnability evidence.
