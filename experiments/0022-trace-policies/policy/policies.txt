Policy P-DOWNWARD-001: Required downward decomposition coverage

Baseline: the exact source and policy files in the Git commit containing this experiment; pass, coverage-fail, and cycle-fail are named snapshots in that commit.
Scope: requirement IDs listed in downward-scope.tsv.
Rule: each row marked required has at least one incoming decomposes relationship; a waived row carries its rationale in the same authoritative policy source.
Rationale: formal trace review must detect selected higher-level requirements that silently lose all lower-level coverage.
False-positive risk: intentionally undecomposed, externally implemented, or incomplete repository slices may have no lower-level requirement. Applying this policy without the declared scope is invalid.

Policy P-ACYCLIC-001: Acyclic assurance decomposition

Baseline: the exact source and policy files in the Git commit containing this experiment; pass, coverage-fail, and cycle-fail are named snapshots in that commit.
Scope: the complete selected source set; cycle-scope.tsv must list every requirement in that set exactly once.
Rule: no requirement in scope is reachable from itself by one or more decomposes relationships.
Rationale under test: a finite assurance decomposition may require every path to terminate at a root. This is a boundary-control candidate, not an adopted project rule.
False-positive risk: another project may intentionally use mutually refining requirements or permit cycles for navigation. Source-language 0.2 allows cycles.
