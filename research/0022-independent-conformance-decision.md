# Independent Conformance Decision

Status: Decided for the 0.2 corpus

Date: 2026-08-29

[Experiment 0015](../experiments/0015-independent-conformance/README.md)
produced an interpretation from the normative standard without maintained
implementation guidance. All 16 validity decisions and both complete semantic
inventories agree with the maintained baseline.

Five source-coordinate differences are permitted diagnostic-anchor choices for
construct-level failures. They do not alter validity or semantic interpretation,
and the standard does not promise byte-identical diagnostics.

Make no standard, fixture, or implementation change. Treat this as bounded
implementation-independence evidence, not human usability evidence or proof that
all future language cases are unambiguous.
