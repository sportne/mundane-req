# End-to-End Requirements Pilot Decision

Status: Complete

Date: 2026-08-30

[Experiment 0024](../experiments/0024-vaccine-monitoring-pilot/assessment.md)
executes a hypothetical vaccine equipment monitoring product through source
baselining, 57 requirements, 70 decomposition links, allocation, verification
planning, contextual safety assessment, native-tool checks, a controlled
retention change, review, and rebaselining.

Retain the 0.2 requirement model unchanged. Identity, statement, rationale,
opaque source, one readable allocation, and decomposition were sufficient for
the selected obligation content. Context was indispensable, but context facts,
verification assertions, safety judgments, change authority, and baseline
meaning remained clearer as separately owned artifacts.

The repeated verification workflow now selects a focused analyzer as the next
ecosystem candidate. Manual checks were needed for requirement/activity
references, exact plan binding, coverage, and staleness. Whole-source-set digest
binding was honest but caused irrelevant safety-assessment churn after two
unrelated requirement revisions. Compare binding granularities and fix a
bounded companion contract before implementation.

The pilot remains self-authored and self-reviewed. It cannot establish human
learnability, independent acceptance, product compliance, or certification and
does not reverse the source 1.0 deferral.
